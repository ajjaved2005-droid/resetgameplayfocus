Action Habits

HOW TO IMPROVE PROCRASTINATION

Practical action habits for university students to defeat delay and build momentum.

STEP 01

The 5-Minute Kickstart

Commit to working on your coursework for just five minutes. Getting over startup friction activates natural forward momentum.

STEP 02

Timebox 25-Min Sprints

Work in dedicated, single-task focus blocks followed by brief, genuine rest breaks before mental fatigue builds up.

STEP 03

Remove Low-Effort Distractions

Place your smartphone out of arm's reach and close non-essential browser tabs before starting your assignment.
