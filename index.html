ABOUT RE:SET

RE:SET helps university students recognize digital distractions, understand procrastination, and develop better focus through interactive games.

Identify your daily attention habits across four psychological personas and train your cognitive control through fast, focused mini-games.

Why RE:SET Matters for Students

University life requires sustained deep work, yet constant digital notifications, academic perfectionism, and deadline anxiety create cognitive fatigue.

The Mission: Rather than lecturing students, RE:SET turns self-awareness into experiential gameplay. Each 3-level game challenges a specific executive dysfunction pattern.

Evidence-Based Reflection: At the end of every run, players receive concrete, actionable micro-habits designed specifically for campus schedules.
