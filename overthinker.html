Mindset Reflection

HOW TO IMPROVE OVERTHINKING

Practical reflection habits for university students dealing with loops of overthinking.

01

01 — BREAK INTO 5-MIN STEPS

Tackle only the very first action when coursework feels overwhelming to build immediate forward momentum.

02

02 — LIMIT THINKING TIME

Set a 5-minute timer to choose a topic or outline, then commit firmly to action without second-guessing.

03

03 — PROGRESS OVER PERFECTION

Course-correct while taking active steps rather than waiting for ideal certainty before starting.
