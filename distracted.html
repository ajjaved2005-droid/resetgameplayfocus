Attention Habits

HOW TO IMPROVE DISTRACTION

Practical focus habits for university students navigating daily digital noise.

01

01 — SILENCE NOTIFICATIONS

Put devices on Do Not Disturb and out of arm's reach during active study sessions to eliminate trigger pings.

02

02 — ONE TAB AT A TIME

Keep only the current assignment tab open to avoid cognitive attention residue from switching contexts.

03

03 — PAUSE BEFORE SWITCHING

When an impulse to browse strikes, take a 10-second breath and deliberately redirect your focus back to work.
